PyTorch interpolation arithmetic
--------------------------------
The native depth resize retains the FP32 align-corners evaluation order of
PyTorch 2.7.1 aten/src/ATen/native/cuda/UpSampleBilinear2d.cu.
Source: https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/v2.7.1/aten/src/ATen/native/cuda/UpSampleBilinear2d.cu
See PyTorch-LICENSE.txt. The desktop does not bundle or require the Torch runtime.
